## What FlatMatch changed because of this

| Gap | Feature now in the app |
| --- | --- |
| Portals and Facebook hide brokerage until you are on the phone, and Flatmate.in charges to unlock contact. | Every room card shows rent, deposit, how many months that deposit is, and brokerage. A no-brokers pledge shows brokerage as ₹0. Chat is not a paid unlock. |
| Broker posts survive on NoBroker, the big portals, and Facebook groups. | A "this is a broker" report is counted on the card. Two seed reports sit on the Koramangala token listing so the badge is visible. Flagged people sort below everyone else. |
| Bunkd, Roommit and Flattie all hide the phone. Flatmate.in only offers it as an option, and still sells calls. | Phone and email are not on cards. A line on the card says the number stays hidden until you match. After a match, sharing the number is a separate button. |
| Groups and brokers ask for a token before the visit. Colive at least lets you book a visit. | From a match you can save a visit plan (when, where, a note that you have not paid) and copy it to a friend. The copied text has no phone numbers. |
| Verification is the claim every new app makes, and the big portals' "owner" tag is not trusted. | The deck has a verified-only filter. The badge is still the mock DigiLocker step, labelled as a mock. |
| Lifestyle match is how Flatmate.in, Bunkd, Roommit and Flattie differentiate from MagicBricks. Work-from-home was the missing habit. | The score already covered food, smoking, drinking, pets, guests, day or night hours, cleanliness and language. It now also scores office, hybrid, or work-from-home. |
| Bunkd's active cities, and Flattie's, include Hyderabad, Delhi NCR and Chennai. FlatMatch only had three. | Seeded seekers and rooms in Hyderabad (Madhapur), Delhi NCR (Noida) and Chennai (OMR), plus the city filter. |
| Almost every catalogue is English-only. | A Hindi toggle covers the tab bar, deck labels, the match screen, and the safety lines. The rest of the copy is still English. That is groundwork, not a full translation. |

FlatMatch stays free to browse, match and chat. It does not take a brokerage and it does not sell contact details. Managed stays such as Colive remain a different product: they choose the building and the rules, and you do not choose the person.
